How the value is worked out

1 · Comps fit

TVL explains about 19% of log market-cap variance; the fit is a pricing anchor, not a prediction. Growth is deliberately left out of the regression: its fitted coefficient is ≈ −0.04 and adding it makes held-out error worse.

2 · Token odds

Share of protocols in each age × TVL cell that record a token, smoothed toward the global rate. Older, larger protocols are more likely to record one.
